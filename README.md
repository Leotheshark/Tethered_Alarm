# Tethered_Alarm

This project is driven by a fusion of psychological behavioral design and social accountability.  

--------------Our core objectives are:------------------

--Making Waking Up Fun (Psychological Shift):

For most people, "waking up" is usually associated with "pain" or "struggle," which makes the start of the day feel like a heavy burden. We want to completely re-engineer this morning dread. By transforming the lonely and boring morning routine into a highly interactive mini-game, we aim to turn the act of waking up into an engaging activity that users can actually look forward to.

--Making Waking Up Easier (Social Accountability): 

Instead of relying on individual, often fragile willpower, we make getting out of bed easier through teamwork and peer pressure. When the alarm goes off, you aren't just waking up for yourself—you are waking up to join a collaborative party game with your friends. If a player decides to stay in bed, their in-game character becomes an immovable "dead weight," severely punishing the rest of the team. This introduces a powerful social motivation to wake up promptly.

--Preventing Accidental Dismissal (Cognitive Engagement): 

Traditional alarms are fundamentally flawed because they can be silenced by muscle memory while the user is still half-asleep. We want to ensure users do not turn off the alarm by mistake and fall back asleep. To prevent this, the alarm cannot be silenced until the mission is complete. Solving a puzzle requires thinking, hand-eye coordination, and communication, ensuring the brain is fully active before the alarm is dismissed.
